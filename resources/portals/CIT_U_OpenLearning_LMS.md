# Cebu Institute of Technology – University (CIT-U) PhilNITS Study Portal

## Overview
- **Portal Platform:** OpenLearning LMS
- **Course Title:** PhilNITS v1 Review Course
- **URL:** [CIT-U PhilNITS v1 Portal](https://www.openlearning.com/college.cit-u/courses/philnitsv1/)
- **Provider:** Cebu Institute of Technology – University College of Computer Studies / Engineering
- **Target Audience:** Graduating BSIT candidates and university-sponsored PhilNITS examinees.

## Role in Exam Preparation
1. **Curriculum Alignment:** Contains faculty-curated learning modules, quizzes, and problem sets reflecting CIT-U's proven review methodology for the ITPEC FE Examination.
2. **Supplemental Material:** Used in tandem with the IPA FE Preparation Books (Vols 1 & 2) and past ITPEC examination papers.
3. **Assessment Checkpoints:** Periodic institutional mock exams and module completion requirements set by the university adviser.

## Integration with the Review Application
- Topics and quizzes completed on the OpenLearning portal should feed into the review app's **Mastery State Tracker** (RED/YELLOW/GREEN).
- Any missed questions or difficult concepts encountered during LMS problem sets should be logged directly into the review app's **Error Log** under the corresponding failure taxonomy (`[K]`, `[C]`, `[N]`, `[M]`, `[T]`).
